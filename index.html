Step Into Living History

Our "Back in Time" tour package is a meticulously crafted journey through Negros Island's remarkable historical and cultural legacy. Over four immersive days, you'll explore beautifully preserved ancestral homes, centuries-old churches, colonial landmarks, and heritage-rich towns that showcase the enduring influence of Spanish colonization and the golden age of the sugar industry.

From the "Paris of Negros" in Silay City to the romantic ruins of Talisay, and from Bacolod's vibrant culture to Dumaguete's educational heritage, this tour offers a unique window into the island's diverse historical experiences. You'll walk the same streets as sugar barons and revolutionaries, taste traditional delicacies, and connect with the living history that makes Negros Island truly special.

Full Itinerary

Graph Theory Application

The tour route is designed as a Hamiltonian Cycle - visiting each vertex (destination) exactly once before returning to the start.

This ensures:

  • No repeated locations
  • Optimal route efficiency
  • Complete circuit through all destinations

Route Optimization Details

  • Geographic Logic

    Routes follow clockwise progression around Negros Island to minimize backtracking. Overnight stays anchor each day's endpoint.

  • Time Efficiency

    Maximum 4h travel/day with strategic meal stops. Ferry crossing scheduled for calmest seas (10AM).

  • Heritage Focus

    Prioritizes UNESCO sites in mornings, cultural meals at peak hours, and golden hour photography at key landmarks.

Key Distances & Travel Times

Bacolod-Silay Airport to Bacolod City 15km • 30min
Silay Heritage Tour Route 8km • 45min
Bacolod to Dumaguete via Mabinay 150km • 4h (with stops)
Dumaguete to Valencia Forest Camp 12km • 30min
Sibulan to San Carlos Ferry 15km • 1h (including boarding)

Scope and Limitations

Key Constraints

  • 4-day fixed itinerary with set departure/return points
  • Dependent on ferry schedules for Sibulan-San Carlos crossing
